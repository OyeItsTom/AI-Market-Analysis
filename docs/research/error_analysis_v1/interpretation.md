## Executive findings

1. **P1 (temporal / episode robustness).** [FACT] On SPY, QQQ and IWM the
   `trend_alignment` BULLISH-below / BEARISH-above pattern recurs in at least
   4 of 5 predeclared segments by mean in 10 of 12 h5/h20 cells and by median
   in 11 of 12; no directional state's largest episode holds more than 0.1387
   of its observations. [FACT] The one contrary segment is the same segment,
   2017-2018, in all 8 SPY and QQQ cells (mean and median). [FACT] The
   secondary episode-start view does **not** reproduce the pattern for the
   BEARISH state (review-computed start-view deltas opposite in sign to the
   full view for SPY BEARISH at both horizons and for IWM BEARISH by mean at
   both horizons). [INTERPRETATION] The pattern recurs across multiple
   predeclared segments, but it is not uniformly present (one shared
   contrary period) and it is substantially shaped by within-episode
   observations rather than by the bars on which a state is first declared.
   Of the four predeclared classifications, A (distributed) is the closest,
   but the label alone would overstate uniformity.
2. **P2 (RSI gate).** [FACT] The `opposite` partition is empty on all five
   symbols. RSI removes 31.8 %–62.7 % of the equity trend-directional bars
   (31.8 %–46.1 % of BULLISH, 56.5 %–62.7 % of BEARISH). The retained
   partition moves in the same direction as the parent state's Phase R delta
   in 8 of 12 equity h5/h20 cells by both mean and median (one of them, SPY
   BULLISH h5, within ±0.0003), has mean and median disagreeing in 3, and is
   reversed in 1 (IWM BEARISH h20, retained −0.00376 / −0.00692). [INTERPRETATION] Classification **C — behaves
   differently by symbol, state and horizon**; on the common sample the gate
   does not isolate a stably different subset, and the one sign-uniform
   separation is inside the *removed* bars (BULLISH-trend bars with
   depressed RSI), not the retained ones — and is itself segment-unstable.
3. **P3 (semantics).** [FACT] No evidence of an implementation defect: every
   cross-tab cell that the code makes impossible is 0, the partitions
   recombine exactly, and the vocabulary contract in
   `src/strategies/research.py` explicitly promises no forward direction.
   [INTERPRETATION] The empirical pattern does not violate the documented
   contract; the names BULLISH/BEARISH nevertheless carry a colloquial
   directional connotation that the equity data of this decade ran against,
   and that Phase R's own interpretation already had to disclaim.
4. **P4 (cross-asset).** [FACT] TLT and GLD keep the Phase R sign reversal
   at the full-window level but with h20 magnitudes below 0.003 and weaker
   segment recurrence (TLT h20: 3/5 in both states by mean and median; GLD
   BEARISH h20: 4/5 mean, 3/5 median). [INTERPRETATION] Too small and
   unstable to support a class-level interpretation; no asset-specific
   proposal.
5. **P5 (crossover).** [FACT] 25–30 events per direction per symbol; the
   median spacing between consecutive crossings is 30.5–43 observation bars
   but 11–17 of each symbol's 49–58 gaps are ≤ 20 bars, inside the h20
   window; mean and
   median disagree in sign in 7 of the 20 h5/h20 delta cells.
   [INTERPRETATION] Classification **B — useful mainly as an event /
   architectural fixture**; not retired, not reframed here.
6. **NEUTRAL heterogeneity.** [FACT] In the full window the
   `fast_above_slow, momentum_depressed, contradicts` group sits above the
   parent BULLISH trend sample at h20 on 5 of 5 symbols by mean and median;
   but a review-computed secondary check per segment shows its positivity
   holding in 5/5 segments by mean on only SPY (h5), IWM (h5) and TLT (h20),
   with 2/5 on QQQ (h5) and segment cells of 19–66 observations. [INTERPRETATION]
   Descriptively different in aggregate, not stably so across segments; no

7. **Phase 13B decision: C — documentation hardening only.** No
   hypothesis, threshold, feature or taxonomy change is supported by the
   frozen diagnostics, so no computational Phase 13B experiment is
   justified; the only action supported is a clarification of what

### P1 conclusion

[INTERPRETATION] The equity `trend_alignment` pattern recurs across
multiple predeclared segments (10 of 12 cells at ≥ 4/5 by mean, 11 of 12 by
median), is not held by any dominant run (largest-episode share ≤ 0.1387;
23–32 episodes per state), but is **not uniformly present** — the single
contrary segment is the same segment, 2017-2018, for SPY and QQQ in all 8
cells — and is **substantially shaped by within-episode observations**: the
episode-start view reproduces the BEARISH side in only 1 of 6 equity cells
by mean. Of the four predeclared classifications, A (distributed) is the
closest — it is not C (concentrated) because no segment or run dominates,
and not D (inconclusive) because the segment recurrence is exact and
repeated — but the label alone would overstate uniformity, and the prose
above is the conclusion.

### What does RSI add?

[INTERPRETATION] **C — the gate behaves differently by symbol, state and
horizon.** The RSI gate changes sample membership materially, but the
resulting retained-versus-removed differences are not sufficiently uniform
across symbols, states and horizons to justify a computational change. On
the common sample the retained subset is not a consistently different
subset: it intensifies the parent's Phase R direction in 8 of 12
equity cells (one of them by less than ±0.0003), has mean and median
disagreeing in 3, reverses in 1, and on TLT sits below the parent by mean in
both directional states. What is consistent is not the
retained partition but one removed group — BULLISH-trend bars on which RSI14
was below 45 — which sat above the parent BULLISH sample on all five symbols
at h20. That is a statement about the bars the gate *discards*, and it
mirrors Phase R's neutral-reason finding from the other side of the same
data (see NEUTRAL section below). It does not make RSI "useful" or "useless"
as a feature: the partitions share the SMA20/SMA50 relation with the parent,
the five symbols include three highly co-moving equity ETFs, and the h20
figures are overlap-inflated. Descriptively, the RSI gate mostly reduces the
observation count (by roughly a third of BULLISH and three fifths of BEARISH
bars on the equities) and fragments the runs (median directional episode
length 2 to 7 bars against 22 to 54.5 for the trend states), while the one
sign-uniform difference it exposes lives in what it removes — and that
difference is segment-unstable (NEUTRAL section). Whether RSI14 adds,
removes or duplicates information is not established here in either
direction.

## P3 — State semantics

[FACT] The vocabulary contract (`src/strategies/research.py`, quoted in D6 of
the frozen report): BULLISH means "this deterministic hypothesis classifies
the supplied evidence as bullish-leaning"; it "does **not** mean 'buy', 'this
will rise', or 'this is a profitable signal'". `TrendAlignment` classifies

## Limitations

- **Overlap.** Forward windows overlap: adjacent daily observations share up
  to 4 of 5 future bars at h5 and 19 of 20 at h20. `sample_count`,
  episode-start n and segment n are counts of overlapping windows, not
  independent trials. Every h20 figure above is more overlap-inflated than
  the corresponding h5 figure, and larger h20 magnitudes are not stronger
  evidence.
- **Observation n versus episode n.** The level states arrive in 23 to 32
  episodes per symbol (52 to 130 for the momentum states); a segment cell of
  365 observations in 4 episodes (SPY BULLISH 2017-2018) describes four
  stretches of history. The episode-start view (23–32 bars) trades overlap
  for a sample so small that a few 20-bar returns set the sign; neither
  count is an effective sample size and no correction was applied.
- **Shared feature.** `trend_alignment` and `momentum_in_trend_context`
  read the same SMA20/SMA50 relation and `trend_crossover` is its
  transition. Agreement between them is one observation seen twice, not
  replication; the D2 partitions are subsets of one another's samples by
  construction.
- **RAW basis.** All returns are on unadjusted prices; SPY, QQQ, IWM and TLT
  distributions sit inside the forward windows on both sides of every
  comparison. Nothing here says what an adjusted-basis study would show;
  that study remains deferred as a separate version.
- **Retrospective data.** The source bars were retrieved from Yahoo on
  2026-09-16 and are reproducible only against the frozen bytes; they are
  not a point-in-time vendor record and not the prospective evidence of the
  Phase 12 ledger.
- **One decade, five correlated instruments.** The three equity ETFs co-move;
  "5 of 5 symbols" is closer to three votes than five. The segments are
  five two-year blocks of one regime set; the matched unconditional drift
  that every delta is measured against was positive for equities and gold
  and negative for TLT in this window.
- **Decision aids are descriptive.** The 4-of-5 and 0.25 references are
  predeclared reading aids. Meeting or missing them is reported, never used
  as a pass/fail, and never called significant, validated or robust.
- **No profitability, no significance.** No position, cost, slippage,
  turnover, capital or execution model exists in Phase R or 13A; no p-value,
  confidence interval, dependence correction or multiple-comparison
  correction was computed. Nothing above is a claim of profitability,
  tradability, alpha, edge, strategy quality, accuracy or statistical
  significance.
- **Review-computed values.** The episode-start deltas, removed percentages,
  crossover positive counts and the per-segment reason-group table were
  computed by the reviewer from frozen rows and are labelled as such; they
  are traceable but are not frozen diagnostics.

## Phase 13B decision

**C — documentation hardening only. No computational Phase 13B experiment
is justified.**

Because the supported action changes no computation, no fingerprint, no
hypothesis and no artifact semantics, it is better described as
documentation hardening than as a Phase 13B experiment; "Phase 13B" in the
sense of ADR 0012 (one controlled change to a hypothesis) is not opened by
this evidence.

[INTERPRETATION] Rationale, by question:

- P1 found the equity pattern recurring across segments but not uniform,
  small at h5, sharing a single contrary period and absent at episode
  starts on the BEARISH side.
  That is a description to record, not a defect to fix; nothing in it points
  to a threshold, feature or window that would be *expected* to behave
  differently.
- P2 found the RSI gate inconsistent across symbol, state and horizon on
  the common sample. Neither removing the gate nor tightening it is
  supported; the one consistent cell lies in the removed bars and is
  segment-unstable.
- P3 found no implementation defect and a vocabulary whose connotation
  outran its contract — the only finding with a change that is small,
  computation-free and evidence-backed.
- P4 found TLT/GLD too small and unstable for any class-specific proposal.
- P5 found crossover an event fixture; the methodology forbids retiring or
  reframing it here and nothing argues for doing so later on this evidence.
- NEUTRAL heterogeneity is real in aggregate and unstable by segment; a
  taxonomy change is not justified.

Scope of the documentation hardening, nominated here and not implemented:

- **What is ambiguous.** The contract in `src/strategies/research.py`
  defines BULLISH/BEARISH as structural classifications, but the names
  carry a colloquial forward-direction connotation; the Phase R and 13A
  results show that, on SPY/QQQ/IWM in 2015–2024, forward returns ran
  against that connotation in most predeclared segments, so a reader who
  meets the names without the contract is invited to a reading the
  contract never guaranteed.
- **Where clarification should later appear.** Near the point of use: the
  `TrendAlignment` and `MomentumInTrendContext` docstrings (that the states
  name an SMA20/SMA50 configuration, with RSI14 read against it), the
  research documentation that introduces the vocabulary, and wherever the
  Phase 12 outcome layer surfaces the state names — with a pointer to the
  frozen Baseline Study v1 / Error Analysis v1 results as the reference for
  what followed those configurations on this universe and decade.
- **What must not change.** No `ResearchState` value, reason code,
  parameter, threshold, feature, fingerprint, horizon, universe, window,
  logic reversal, new state or artifact changes; no label is reversed or
  renamed.

Option A (one controlled candidate) was rejected because every computational candidate on this evidence would be
chosen because one cell looked attractive, which is the failure ADR 0012
exists to prevent; option D (more diagnostics) was rejected because the
additional diagnostic that would be wanted — the per-segment reason-group
view — was computed as a secondary check above and did not change the
answer; option B (nothing) understates P3.

## Future validation guardrail

[FACT] Data from 2025-03-01 onward remains chronologically untouched. Phase
R read bars through 2025-02-28 only to evaluate outcomes of late-2024
observations; Phase 13A refused any source row on or after 2025-01-01, read
no provider, and this review read nothing beyond the frozen artifacts.

Future validation, when a later phase reserves that window: one
pre-specified candidate; one run; after a methodology freeze; with a sample
long enough for the level states (the crossover states would need decades);
with no repeated peeking and no candidate revision after the window is
opened. The Phase 13A evidence shows that sign recurrence was not
universal; for example, 2017-2018 was contrary in several equity cells. A
future validation should therefore allow for sign reversal rather than
treating the retrospective direction as guaranteed, and that allowance
belongs in the pre-specification, not in a post-hoc explanation.
